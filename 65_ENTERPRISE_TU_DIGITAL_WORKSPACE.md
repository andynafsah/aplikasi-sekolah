# Enterprise Tata Usaha & Digital Workspace Engine (SIMAS Pro)

Executed successfully and integrated into the single-tenant school & pesantren ERP platform (SIMAS Pro).

## Key Features & Modules
1. **Dashboard Tata Usaha**: Real-time statistics on total students, santri, employees, incoming letters, outgoing letters, pending dispositions, legal document expiration alerts, and agenda items.
2. **Surat-Menyurat (Correspondence & Disposition)**:
   - Incoming & Outgoing letters registration with automated numbering format.
   - Digital disposition routing (Instruksi, Catatan, Penerima disposisi).
   - E-Sign (Tanda Tangan Elektronik) & QR Code generation and verification.
   - Letter templates and dynamic variable substitution.
3. **Arsip Digital & Retensi (Digital Archive)**:
   - Categorized document archiving with retention schedules and shelf/box tracking.
   - Secure preview, download, and versioning.
4. **Administrasi Siswa & Pegawai**:
   - Comprehensive student & employee records, admissions, graduation, mutations, and documentation (Akta, KK, KTP, Ijazah, SK).
5. **Layanan & Buku Tamu (Guest Book & Expedition)**:
   - Digital guest check-in (Buku Tamu Tamadun) and expedition book tracking.
6. **Legalitas & Reminder**:
   - Institutional legal documents tracking (Izin Operasional, Akta Yayasan, Akta Pendirian) with automated expiration warnings.
7. **Reporting & Print Center**:
   - Export to PDF, Excel, CSV, and direct printable views for all administrative data.
